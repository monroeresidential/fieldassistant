# Field Assistant Roadmap

One repo, three folders (`api/`, `ios/`, `web/`). Phone is the product; the web app is phase 2 of the product and starts after the Jan 8, 2027 gate. Each milestone below gets its own detailed plan in this folder when it starts. Dates are from the spec's delivery plan.

**Spec:** `procore-field-assistant-spec.md` as amended by `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-v1-decisions.md`.

| # | Milestone | Dates | Plan | Exit criterion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Auth, contract, scaffold | Oct 27 – Nov 7, 2026 | `2026-10-22-phase-1-auth-contract-scaffold.md` | User signs in on the phone, picks a company and project, sees the project's RFI list from the backend. `api/openapi.yaml` generates the Go server and the Swift client in CI. |
| 2 | RFI extract + review | Nov 9 – Nov 20 | written at the end of milestone 1 | A dictated RFI becomes a reviewable draft with resolved IDs and posts to the sandbox without attachments. |
| 3 | Photos, uploads, offline | Nov 23 – Dec 4 | written at the end of milestone 2 | Attachments reach Procore via Direct File Upload; a submit with no signal completes exactly once when connectivity returns. |
| 4 | Hardening, TestFlight | Dec 7 – Dec 18 | written at the end of milestone 3 | Permission errors, token revocation, configurable validations; first build to the 118 W Illinois team. |
| 5 | Site pilot | Dec 21 – Jan 8, 2027 | no code plan; weekly audit review, eval set seeded from corrections | Two weeks of daily use, no duplicate submissions, under 20% of drafts needing more than two corrections. |
| — | **Gate review** | **Jan 8, 2027** | | Milestone 5 criterion passes. |
| 6 | Web app (phase 2 of the product) | from Jan 11 | written after the gate | Same loop on Cloudflare Pages against the unchanged backend. |
| 7 | Feature 2: Observations | Jan 11 – Jan 22 | written after the gate | Observation module in `api/` and `ios/` (and `web/` once it exists). |
| 8 | Feature 3: Punch items + batch mode | Jan 25 – Feb 12 | written after milestone 7 | Batch mode is desktop-first, so it depends on milestone 6. |

**What each later milestone adds to the codebase (so milestone 1 leaves room for it):**

- Milestone 2: `api/internal/llm`, `api/internal/pipeline` (drafts, lookup cache), `api/internal/features/rfi` (schema, prompt.md, extract), `lookup_cache` and `drafts` and `audit_events` tables, `/projects/{id}/lookups/{kind}`, `/drafts` endpoints; iOS `Core/Capture`, `Core/Review`, `Core/Lookups`, `Features/RFI`.
- Milestone 3: `api/internal/jobs` (River workers), `attachments` table, `/attachments` and `/drafts/{id}/submit` and `/jobs/{id}`; iOS `Core/Drafts` (SwiftData queue, BackgroundTasks).
- Milestone 4: `/projects/{id}/features` permission probing, 422 field-error mapping, Fastlane `beta` lane wired to App Store Connect.
